# Case 01: A polished headline can still change the facts

**Status: AI-assisted demonstration draft. Leon has not yet reviewed or approved the judgments below.**

This original fictional exercise is a learning and discussion sample, not a client assignment, a record of paid work, or a published news story. The source note, candidate summaries, and proposed analysis were created with AI assistance. Candidates A and B are constructed examples, not a benchmark of named models.

## Task

Write a short Chinese news headline and one-sentence summary using only the source note. Preserve numbers, conditions, attribution, and the difference between a proposal and a decision.

## Fictional source note

A community center said Tuesday that it **plans** to offer **up to 120** free computer-training places **next spring**, **if grant funding is approved**. Registration has **not opened**. The center estimates that **about 40** places could include Chinese-language support; staffing is **not confirmed**. No city agency has announced or approved the program in this exercise.

## Candidate A

**社区中心拟于明春开设免费电脑培训，资助尚待批准**

该中心周二表示，若资助获批，计划明春提供最多120个免费培训名额，其中约40个可能提供中文支持；报名尚未开始，人员安排仍未确定。

## Candidate B

**市府批准120个免费电脑培训名额，40个中文班即日起报名**

市府周二宣布免费电脑培训项目，120人将获得培训，其中40人可参加中文班，居民现在即可报名。

## Proposed review

**Preference: A, for source fidelity.** This is the draft analysis, not Leon's personal assessment. B is readable and concise, but changes the actor, approval status, capacity, language support, and registration timing. Fluency cannot compensate for those changes.

| Check | Source evidence | A | B | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Attribution | Community center said | Preserves center attribution | Substitutes city government | Assigns responsibility to an unsupported source |
| Approval | Plans; grant approval required | Retains the condition | Claims approval | Turns a contingent plan into a decision |
| Capacity | Up to 120 places | 最多120个 | 120人将获得培训 | Converts a ceiling into a guaranteed outcome |
| Language support | About 40 places could include support | 约40个可能提供中文支持 | 40个中文班 / 40人可参加中文班 | Headline changes places to classes; both versions imply a firmer offering |
| Timing | Next spring; registration not open | Keeps both points | Says registration starts now | Could prompt readers to act on nonexistent availability |
| Staffing | Not confirmed | Preserves uncertainty | Omits it while promising classes | Removes a qualification relevant to language support |

## Decision rule used here

1. Check every actor, number, date, and claim against the source.
2. Mark unsupported claims separately from awkward wording.
3. Treat changes to eligibility, availability, approval, or timing as major factual problems when they could alter a reader's action.
4. Prefer the answer that preserves meaning; then improve clarity and length.
5. Do not infer facts that neither the source nor the task supplies.

These are proposed review rules for this exercise, not a claim about any platform's confidential rubric.

## A tighter alternative

**社区中心拟开免费电脑培训，仍待资助获批**

中心称，计划明春提供最多120个名额，约40个可能有中文支持；报名和人员安排尚未确定。

**Trade-off:** This shorter version loses the Tuesday attribution date and softens “registration has not opened” into a broader statement. If that precision matters, keep Candidate A rather than shortening further.
