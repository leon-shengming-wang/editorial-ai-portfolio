What this case is designed to show

  1. Track intent across turns. Do not reward or punish length without checking what the user currently needs.
  2. Preserve strengths while describing defects. A preference does not make one candidate flawless.
  3. Separate observation from inference. Repetitive text is observable; unnatural vocal delivery is not established without audio.
  4. Check core facts first. A direct and polished answer can still be unusable when its key fact is wrong.
